Spaghetti carbonara

Recipe Overview

This classic Italian pasta is rich , creamy and full of flavor

it takes only 20 minutes to prepare and is prefare for a quick meal (serve 2 people)

Ingredients

  • 200g Spaghetti
  • 2 Eggs
  • 100g Pancetta
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
  • salt and black paper

cooking steps

  1. Boil Spaghetti in salted water until al dente
  2. cook pancetta until crispy
  3. combine everything with drained pasta while bot
  4. season with salt anf paper

Tips

Make sure the pasta is hot when you mix in the egg and cheese mixture for a creamy texture
